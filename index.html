01 // Focus & Domain

About & Philosophy

I am Akhil Seshan, a Senior Software Engineer at FactSet, based in Thodupuzha, Kerala.

My engineering focus centers on Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Deep Learning. Currently, I serve as a core contributor to FactSet's greenfield Portfolio Commentary initiative—integrating LLMs into production Go microservices to generate human-grade financial analysis and power conversational AI assistants.

I specialize in end-to-end GenAI reliability: engineering prompt tuning frameworks, continuous evaluation benchmarks, narrative compliance checks, and vector retrieval pipelines using Weaviate and modern semantic protocols (MCP).

When I step away from code and neural networks, I am drawn to travel and photography. Exploring unfamiliar landscapes and capturing perspectives sharpens my appreciation for composition, visual minimalism, and precision—qualities that directly influence how I design software architectures.
